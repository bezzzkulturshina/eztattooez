About me

Olena Zadorozhna — tattoo artist from Moncalieri, Turin.

My name is Elena Zadorozhna, and I am a tattoo artist with over 9 years of experience. For me, every tattoo is a unique project, created to tell something personal and become a harmonious part of the body. I specialize in Realism, Fine Line, Concept Tattoo, and Lettering, with particular attention to composition, aesthetics, and detail. I don’t simply work from a template. I start with your idea, develop it, and create a personalized design tailored to your anatomy and the placement of the tattoo. My goal is to create tattoos that are not only beautiful to look at, but that maintain character, balance, and identity over time. **Individuality. Aesthetics. Quality.** My studio is located in Moncalieri (Turin). A place where your idea takes shape and becomes something truly unique.

The studio is in Moncalieri, but clients come from all over Turin and nearby cities. Follow my work on Instagram — there I post fresh tattoos, sketches and available ideas.

FAQ

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by filling out the booking form or contacting me directly on Instagram. I’ll get back to you as soon as possible.

How much does a tattoo cost?

The price depends on the size, placement, style, and complexity of the tattoo. Contact me with your idea for a personalized quote.

Do you create custom designs?

Yes. I create custom tattoo designs based on your ideas, references, and personal preferences.

What tattoo styles do you specialize in?

I specialize in all styles.

Does getting a tattoo hurt?

Pain levels depend on the placement and your individual tolerance. I always do my best to make the experience as comfortable as possible.

How should I prepare for my session?

Get a good night's sleep, eat a proper meal beforehand, stay hydrated, and avoid alcohol before your appointment.

Is the tattoo process safe and sterile?

Absolutely. I use single-use sterile cartridges, medical-grade disinfectants, and proper barrier protection throughout every session.

How long does a tattoo session take?

Session times vary depending on the size, placement, style, and complexity of the tattoo. The estimated time will be discussed before your appointment.

Where are you located?

You can find me at Via Giacomo Matteotti 4, Moncalieri, Turin, Italy.
